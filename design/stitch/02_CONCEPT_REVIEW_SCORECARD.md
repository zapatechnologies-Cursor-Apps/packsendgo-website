## 1. Scoring definitions

| Score | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Unacceptable — fails requirement; blocks progression |
| 2 | Weak — significant revision required before consideration |
| 3 | Adequate — meets minimum but lacks distinction or polish |
| 4 | Strong — meets requirements with minor revision opportunities |
| 5 | Excellent — clearly exceeds requirements; ready for progression with minimal changes |

Score each criterion independently. Multiply score by weight to calculate weighted total.
