# PackSendGo Concept Review Scorecard

Product Owner review scorecard for comparing PackSendGo Phase 1A Google Stitch homepage concepts.

**Authority:** `design/stitch/00_STITCH_CONCEPT_BRIEF.md`, `docs/02_DESIGN_AND_3D_AUTHORITY.md`

Use this scorecard after all three directions, quotation-flow concept and warehouse-page concept are exported. Phase 1A is not complete until one direction is explicitly selected by the Product Owner.

---

## 1. Scoring definitions

| Score | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Unacceptable — fails requirement; blocks progression |
| 2 | Weak — significant revision required before consideration |
| 3 | Adequate — meets minimum but lacks distinction or polish |
| 4 | Strong — meets requirements with minor revision opportunities |
| 5 | Excellent — clearly exceeds requirements; ready for progression with minimal changes |

Score each criterion independently. Multiply score by weight to calculate weighted total.

**Calculation:** Weighted score = Σ (criterion score × criterion weight percentage ÷ 100)
