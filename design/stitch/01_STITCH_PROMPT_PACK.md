# PackSendGo Stitch Prompt Pack

Ready-to-use Google Stitch prompts for PackSendGo Phase 1A concept exploration.

**Authority:** `design/stitch/00_STITCH_CONCEPT_BRIEF.md`, `docs/02_DESIGN_AND_3D_AUTHORITY.md`

**Rules for every prompt:**

- Use British English.
- Do not invent business facts, statistics, testimonials, addresses, contact details or courier relationships.
- Do not show lorem ipsum; use approved working copy or clearly labelled placeholders.
- Maintain conversion focus: primary CTA is Get a tailored quote; secondary is Tour our warehouse.
- Create an original layout; do not copy a third-party website.
- All marketing copy remains subject to Product Owner approval.

---

## Prompt A — Direction One: Precision Warehouse

### Visual character

Dark industrial elegance. Strong warehouse geometry. Controlled lighting. Precise lines. Operational confidence. Restrained electric cobalt accent. Restrained signal-lime CTA accent. Cinematic but believable. Feels like a premium physical operation supported by technology — not a software dashboard.

### Desktop homepage instruction

```

VISUAL CHARACTER: Precision Warehouse — dark industrial elegance, strong geometry, controlled warehouse lighting, precise lines, operational confidence. Restrained signal-lime for primary CTA only — lime must not dominate.

LAYOUT: Full-width hero left-aligned copy, right-aligned warehouse visual placeholder. Generous whitespace. Strong editorial sans-serif typography (Geist, Manrope or Inter style). 12-column grid, 1440px artboard.

REQUIRED SECTIONS (top to bottom):
1. Header — logo placeholder, nav: Home, Services, How It Works, Our Warehouse, About, Get a Quote
2. Premium hero — headline: "From shelf to doorstep, handled." Supporting: "Flexible ecommerce fulfilment, warehousing, pick and pack, and dispatch for growing brands." Primary CTA button: "Get a tailored quote". Secondary text link: "Tour our warehouse". Proposition and CTAs must remain readable before 3D loads.
3. Stylised Spline warehouse placeholder — integrated with or immediately following hero. Cutaway showing goods-in, storage, picking, packing, dispatch zones. Label: "Spline warehouse experience — design placeholder". HTML-style stage controls: Goods in | Store | Pick | Pack | Send. Static poster state. All text outside visual. This is the only stylised 3D experience on the page.
4. Trust strip — 3–4 structural capability placeholders (no fake statistics)
5. Core services — select the most suitable homepage services from the complete authorised public list below. Use a curated subset for clarity; do not display all twelve simultaneously. 6. How fulfilment works — horizontal process line: Receive → Store → Pick → Pack → Dispatch
7. Real warehouse evidence — photography placeholder frames labelled "Real warehouse imagery — pending". Separate from stylised Spline hero; no second 3D experience.
8. Platform support — Shopify, WooCommerce, Amazon, eBay as text labels only (no integration claim)
9. Customer categories — New ecommerce brands, Growing brands, Marketplace sellers, Subscription businesses, Importers
10. Quotation CTA band — "Ready to discuss your fulfilment requirements?" + Get a tailored quote button
```

## Prompt D — Quotation flow

```
Design a five-step quotation enquiry flow for PackSendGo fulfilment services.

CONTEXT: Premium dark-led website. Mobile-first. Lead qualification only — NO instant pricing, NO binding quote, NO payment, NO account creation.

COLOURS: Midnight graphite background, charcoal form surfaces, warm ivory text, muted steel labels, cobalt focus states, signal-lime submit button (restrained).

STEPS (show all five as separate screens or one scrollable flow):
1. Contact and company — Name, Company, Email, Telephone, Website URL (optional), Country, Preferred contact method
2. Business and sales channels — Business stage, Product category, Current fulfilment, Sales channel checkboxes (Shopify, WooCommerce, Amazon, eBay, TikTok Shop, Etsy, Other, Custom)
3. Orders and stock — Monthly order range (with "Not sure"), SKU count, Items per order, Stock volume, Storage type
4. Delivery and additional requirements — Delivery regions (UK, Europe, International). Additional services checkboxes (enquiry requirements only; operational availability subject to PackSendGo confirmation): Branded packaging, Inserts, Labelling, Barcoding, Bundling, Returns, Rework, Quality checks, Subscription-box assembly, Other requirements. Do not include Kitting.
5. Review and consent — Summary, Additional notes, Privacy consent (required), Marketing consent (optional), Accuracy confirmation, Submit button

REQUIREMENTS:
- Progress indicator showing step 1–5
- Clear Back and Continue buttons
- "I'm not sure" on range fields
- Accessible labels, visible focus, error state example
- Success state: quotation reference displayed, message that PackSendGo will respond manually
- Plain language throughout

PROHIBITED: Price calculator, instant quote, payment fields, login, file upload, lorem ipsum, fake response times.

OUTPUT: Desktop (1440px) and mobile (390px) quotation flow screens.
```
