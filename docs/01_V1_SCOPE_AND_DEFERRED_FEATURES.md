# PackSendGo V1 Scope and Deferred Features

## 1. Release objective

PackSendGo V1 is a premium public marketing and lead-generation website.

The release must explain PackSendGo’s fulfilment services, establish trust in the warehouse operation and convert qualified visitors into quotation enquiries.

It is not a customer fulfilment platform.

## 2. V1 audiences

The website should serve:

- New ecommerce brands
- Growing online retailers
- Shopify merchants
- WooCommerce merchants
- Marketplace sellers
- Subscription businesses
- Importers
- Wholesalers
- Established businesses changing fulfilment provider

## 3. V1 page scope

### Home

Purpose:

- Explain the business immediately
- Introduce the fulfilment process
- Display the premium warehouse visual
- Build trust
- Send visitors towards the quotation form

### Services

Purpose:

- Present the approved PackSendGo services
- Explain customer outcomes
- Avoid excessive technical detail
- Route visitors towards a quotation

### How It Works

Purpose:

- Explain the complete operational journey
- Show the stages from stock receipt to dispatch
- Reduce uncertainty
- Demonstrate operational control

### Our Warehouse

Purpose:

- Show the real operation
- Host photography, video and optional walkthrough content
- Explain goods-in, storage, picking, packing, dispatch and returns
- Build trust through evidence

### About PackSendGo

Purpose:

- Explain the joint venture
- Present leadership and operating philosophy
- Communicate service standards
- Introduce the real team when approved assets exist

### Get a Quote

Purpose:

- Collect qualified fulfilment requirements
- Explain what happens after submission
- Provide a low-friction conversion journey

### Legal pages

The initial legal pages are:

- Privacy Policy
- Cookie Policy
- Website Terms

Service terms may be added when operational contracts and legal review are ready.

## 4. V1 quotation form

The quotation journey shall collect, where relevant:

### Contact information

- Name
- Company
- Email
- Telephone
- Website or ecommerce store
- Country
- Preferred contact method

### Business profile

- Business stage
- Product category
- Current fulfilment arrangement
- Desired start date
- Primary reason for changing provider

### Order profile

- Approximate monthly order volume
- Number of SKUs
- Average items per order
- Seasonal volume changes
- Expected growth

### Stock profile

- Approximate stock quantity
- Pallet or shelving requirements
- Average product dimensions
- Average product weight
- Fragile or specialist handling needs

### Sales channels

- Shopify
- WooCommerce
- Amazon
- eBay
- TikTok Shop
- Etsy
- Other marketplace
- Custom ecommerce platform

Displaying a platform does not claim a live integration unless separately confirmed.

### Delivery profile

- UK delivery
- European delivery
- International delivery
- Average parcel size
- Average parcel weight
- Tracked delivery requirement
- Special courier requirement

### Additional services

- Branded packaging
- Inserts
- Labelling
- Barcoding
- Bundling
- Returns
- Rework
- Quality checks
- Subscription-box assembly
- Other requirements

### Final information

- Additional notes
- Consent to data processing
- Confirmation that supplied information is accurate

## 5. V1 administrative handling

V1 does not require a complete admin dashboard.

The system shall:

- Store quotation records
- Give each submission a unique reference
- Record submission timestamps
- Record email-delivery attempts
- Allow the team to receive the enquiry by email
- Preserve structured data for future pricing automation

A minimal protected internal quotation view may only be added if separately approved.

## 6. V1 design scope

The design shall include:

- Desktop homepage
- Mobile homepage
- Desktop quotation flow
- Mobile quotation flow
- Services page
- How It Works page
- Warehouse page
- About page
- Legal-page template
- Header
- Footer
- Buttons
- Form controls
- Service cards
- Process components
- Warehouse-stage controls
- Loading states
- Error states
- Success state
- Responsive behaviour

## 7. V1 3D scope

The approved V1 3D scope is:

- One main stylised Spline warehouse scene
- Five to seven selectable warehouse stages
- Limited purposeful animation
- Normal HTML labels and controls
- Poster image before loading
- Mobile fallback
- Reduced-motion fallback
- Lazy loading
- Off-screen pause where practical

The following are excluded:

- Custom warehouse simulator
- Real-time inventory visualisation
- Multiplayer 3D
- Complex game controls
- First-person navigation
- User-controlled machinery
- Real-time warehouse data
- Pricing generated from 3D interaction

## 8. Content requirements

V1 requires:

- Approved business description
- Approved services
- Approved contact information
- Approved warehouse address
- Real warehouse photographs
- Real operational facts
- Approved ecommerce-platform support
- Approved delivery coverage
- Privacy and legal information
- Clear quotation-response expectations

Where final facts are unavailable, visible placeholders may exist in planning documents but must not appear in production.

## 9. Launch exclusions

V1 excludes:

- Customer accounts
- Order-management portal
- Inventory portal
- Shipment portal
- Live tracking
- Carrier integrations
- Store integrations
- Online payments
- Automatic quotation calculation
- OpenAI integration
- Chatbot
- Mobile applications
- Customer document uploads
- Public pricing calculator
- Live chat hosted by PackSendGo
- Complex CMS
- Large blog programme
- Advanced analytics dashboard
- Referral programme
- Partner portal
- Multi-language support unless separately approved

## 11. Release success criteria

PackSendGo V1 succeeds when:

- Visitors understand the service quickly.
- The site looks substantially more modern than conventional fulfilment websites.
- Real warehouse evidence establishes trust.
- The site performs well on mobile.
- The 3D experience enhances rather than delays conversion.
- Visitors can submit qualified quotation requests easily.
- PackSendGo receives complete structured enquiries.
- No unsupported operational claims are displayed.
- The architecture leaves a clean path to future AI-assisted quoting.
